Learn about Luke's Cookies

When I started Luke's Cookies, I had no idea where to begin. I knew I loved to bake desserts of all varieties, but I wasn't sure how to turn that passion into a business. After careful consideration of many career paths, I decided to specialize in selling gormet cookies and other baked goods. I now offer four permanent cookie flavours, with one monthly special flavor. You can purchase my cookies online or in person at the University of Rio Grande's campus. Thank you for your support!
